Hazard Assessment

Ref Hazard identified Who might be harmed & how Existing control measures LIScore Residual risk acceptable? / further action
1 Slips, trips & falls — oil spills, wet floor, trailing cables, entrance step Client & therapist — bruising, sprains, fractures from a fall Floor kept clear and dry; spills wiped immediately; oils/towels stored tidily; cables routed away from walkways; non-slip flooring; studio and entrance well lit. 236 Yes — low/ALARP. Monitor; re-check lighting in winter months.
2 Access to/from studio — garden path, uneven ground, poor light, ice/leaves Client — slip or trip approaching or leaving the studio Path kept even, swept and clear; outdoor lighting for evening appointments; grit/salt applied in icy conditions; clients shown the route and advised on arrival. 224 Yes — low/ALARP. Dynamic check of path condition before each appointment in poor weather.
3 Massage couch failure or fall from couch Client — fall, impact injury Couch checked before each use and kept within rated weight limit; height set for safe mounting/dismounting; client assisted on and off; client never left unattended on the couch. 236 Yes — low/ALARP. Couch serviced per manufacturer guidance; log any defect and remove from use.
4 Electrical equipment — heater, lamp, towel warmer, chargers Client & therapist — electric shock, burn, fire Visual check of plugs/leads before use; PAT testing where applicable; sockets not overloaded; damaged items withdrawn; equipment switched off when not in use. 133 Yes — very low/ALARP. Annual PAT check.
5 Hot items — towel warmer, heat packs, wax Client — burns/scalds, especially where sensation is reduced Temperature checked before skin contact; barrier/towel used; not applied over broken skin or areas of reduced sensation; client asked to confirm comfort. 122 Yes — very low/ALARP.
6 Treating a contraindicated condition or undisclosed health issue Client — worsening of an existing condition; adverse health event Health screening completed before first treatment and reviewed each visit; contraindications checked; GP clearance sought where required; treatment adapted, localised or declined as appropriate; therapist works within scope of training. 248 Yes — low/ALARP. Keep screening and CPD current; refer on where appropriate.
7 Adverse reaction — soreness, bruising, dizziness, allergy to medium Client — discomfort, allergic reaction, faintness Allergy check before selecting oil/wax; pressure matched to feedback; client monitored throughout and can stop at any time; aftercare advice given; medical referral if needed. 224 Yes — low/ALARP.
8 Manual-handling / positioning injury during treatment Client & therapist — musculoskeletal strain Safe therapist posture and technique; couch at correct working height; client positioned and draped for comfort and support; regular breaks between clients. 224 Yes — low/ALARP.
9 Cross-infection — contact surfaces, linens, hands Client & therapist — transmission of infection Fresh couch roll/clean towels per client; couch and surfaces cleaned and disinfected between clients; hands washed/sanitised before and after; cuts covered; therapist does not treat when unwell. 224 Yes — low/ALARP.
10 Contagious illness (client or therapist) Client & therapist — spread of infection Clients asked not to attend if feverish or contagious and appointments rescheduled without penalty; therapist cancels if unwell. 122 Yes — very low/ALARP.
11 Fire Client & therapist — burns, smoke inhalation Clear, unobstructed exit; smoke alarm tested regularly; sockets not overloaded; heaters/candles never left unattended; client made aware of the exit on arrival. 144 Yes — low/ALARP. Test alarm monthly; keep extinguisher/blanket accessible.
12 Medical emergency / client becomes unwell Client — delayed treatment of a serious event Current first-aid certificate held; first-aid kit on site; emergency contact recorded on screening form; charged phone to hand to call 999; appropriate insurance in place. 144 Yes — low/ALARP. First-aid certificate kept in date.
13 Lone working — treating clients alone Therapist — vulnerability if an incident occurs with no one present Appointments logged; a trusted contact knows the daily schedule; phone charged and accessible; new clients screened; right to decline or end a session reserved. 236 Yes — low/ALARP. Keep check-in arrangement with trusted contact.
All hazards above are assessed as low or very low (ALARP) with the existing controls in place. Alongside this generic assessment, a dynamic risk assessment is carried out for each appointment — if the activity, the premises, the equipment or conditions change, controls are reviewed and adjusted so risks remain ALARP.

Review (Step 5)

This assessment is reviewed at least annually, and sooner following any accident or near miss, a change to the premises, equipment or working practices, or where there is reason to doubt that the controls remain effective.
